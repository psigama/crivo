Technical detail

Crivo is a platform for research on the phenomena of anomalous cognition. Mediumship, taken to be a branch of anomalous cognition, is known in the literature by the technical name anomalous information reception, or AIR (recepção anômala de informação). The platform has four protocols: two mediumship experiments and two extrasensory perception tests, all of them under Protocols.

The experiments do not exist to prove that mediumship is real, nor that it is not. They exist so that the answer, whatever it is, can be defended before a skeptic, a research ethics committee or a reviewer who reads the study years later.

Protocol P1-automático · mediumship experiment

An experiment that runs entirely through the website

Someone who has lost a person gives that person's first name and answers multiple-choice questions about them. A medium, drawn at random, receives only the first name and answers the same questions. The system compares the medium's answers with the answers about the right person and with those about another, similar person, to measure how much chance explains. Nobody judges whether a sentence "fits", and nobody on the team stands in between: from sign-up to result, everything is automatic.
