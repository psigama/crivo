Run a study

Research groups, spiritist centers and universities can run the platform on their own infrastructure: it is free software and starts with a single command, with no container and no cloud service. Before it begins, a study needs:

  • a research question and an analysis plan, written down;
  • screening criteria for mediums and for sitters, defined beforehand;
  • approval by a Brazilian research ethics committee (CEP/Plataforma Brasil), if academic publication is intended;
  • clarity about the funding, declared along with the study.

Audit it

Skeptics are welcome, and not out of politeness: a protocol nobody has tried to break is not worth much. The timestamps and the revealed seed can be checked by anyone, and the verification package of each completed study makes it possible to redo the audit trail, the draw, the scoring and the analysis from outside. See how to audit it.
