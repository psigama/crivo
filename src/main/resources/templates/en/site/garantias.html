What the system guarantees, what it attests and what it does not attest, and what is asked in return of the people who take part.

What the system guarantees

Guarantees enforced by the code, not by good will

In a few words: nobody knows what they should not, the rules are sealed before anything begins, nothing changes afterwards, and every result is published, for or against. Each guarantee below says this in one sentence, and the technical detail is one tap away.

Blinding

Nobody sees what they should not: the medium does not know who made the request, and nobody sees a score before the end.

Technical detail

Each role has its own projections: the forbidden field does not exist in the query, instead of being hidden on the screen. Every rule has a test that tries to break it.

Verifiable draw

The draw can be redone by anyone, and always gives the same result.

Technical detail

A 32-byte seed from the cryptographic generator, expanded with SHA-256 in counter mode, so that the draw can be redone on any machine and operating system.

Immutable preregistration

The rules are written down and sealed before anything begins. Nobody changes the rules midway.

Technical detail

Once frozen, the document does not change — the database refuses. Amendments create a new version, and deviations in execution are recorded separately, also with no deletion.

Append-only trail

Every step goes into a public record that takes no eraser, not even the administrator's.

Technical detail

Each event carries the hash of the previous one. Update and delete are blocked by a trigger in the database, including for the administrator.

Analysis with no way out

The calculation done at the end is the one agreed on at the start. If it is not, that is stated in writing.

Technical detail

The analysis only gets the confirmatory label if the preregistration was frozen and the sample is the declared one. Otherwise it comes out marked exploratory, with the reason beside it.

No AI in the judging

No machine judges the readings: the answers are compared by a simple, public rule.

Technical detail

No model evaluates a reading or assigns a score. The scoring is an equality comparison between options, under a deterministic, published rule.

Declared limits

What Crivo attests, and what it does not attest

It is worth saying this precisely, because it is the difference between a research tool and a seal of authenticity — and because serious institutions in the field have already been criticized for leaving that boundary vague.

The system attests

  • that the preregistration was frozen before the first datum, and what it contained;
  • that the draw was made in that way and can be redone by anyone;
  • that the recorded sequence of events was not altered afterwards;
  • that the analysis that ran is, or is not, the one that had been declared.

The system does not attest

  • that anyone has mediumistic ability, nor the opposite;
  • that a positive result means survival of consciousness;
  • that a participant is honest — no software guarantees that;
  • that the protocol was followed outside what it records.

The platform also does not certify mediums, does not keep a public register of participants and does not broker consultations. It is nobody's showcase. What each protocol's result does not support is declared on that protocol's page, under Protocols.

With the people who take part

What is asked, and what is guaranteed

A blind protocol places real demands on the people who take part — waiting for the end of the study with no feedback at all, answering a closed questionnaire about someone they have lost, or about someone whose first name is all they know. In return, these guarantees are not a promise of good will: they are in the design of the database and in the tests.

What is asked

  • Specific, clearly separated consent for sensitive data, before any collection.
  • Of the medium: accepting conditions that can be uncomfortable — the first name only, a closed questionnaire and no feedback before the end of the study.
  • Of the sitter: answering the questionnaire honestly, about what they knew of the person, and waiting for the end of the study to see the reading.
  • Of whoever runs the study: declaring screening criteria beforehand, not after seeing the results.

What is guaranteed

  • Withdrawal at any time, with no reason given, with removal or anonymization as set out in the participation agreement.
  • Identifiable data encrypted, in a separate schema, reachable by a single module of the system.
  • No personal data in a log, an error message, a file name or an exported package.
  • Feedback planned for the end, with careful language for bereaved people.
  • No evaluation by artificial intelligence, at any stage.
