Vocabulary and protocol

Words that do not presume the answer

Much of the discussion about mediumship gets lost in the vocabulary. Here, each term is chosen to describe what happened, without asserting where the information came from.

Terms

The vocabulary of the study

Leitura · reading
The medium's answers to the questionnaire about one target, knowing only the first name. The Portuguese word renders reading, the term used in the scientific literature. In spiritist vocabulary the closest word would be comunicação, “communication” — the swap is deliberate: “communication” already presupposes that someone is communicating, whereas “reading” describes only what was observed.
Questionário · questionnaire
The versioned list of multiple-choice fields about the deceased person, the same one for the sitter and for the medium, frozen in the preregistration. Each field is a single closed question: there is no free text to interpret, and so nobody judges whether an answer “fits”.
Consulente · sitter
The person who knew the deceased in life and sends the request: the first name and the answers to the questionnaire. In the English-language literature the term is sitter, and that is what to search for in the papers; in Portuguese the project uses consulente, which describes the role without sounding like translated jargon. In the code the identifier stays sitter, under the rule that code is written in English.
Alvo · target
The deceased person named by the sitter. The medium receives only the first name.
Chamariz · decoy
The answers of the other sitter in the pair, against which the same reading is also checked. It is what separates a hit from a common answer: “died of a heart problem” matches a great many people, and it matches the decoy too.
Par · pair
Two requests with a similar profile — same gender, age band and period of death —, by the criteria declared before collection. Each one is the decoy of the other, and the same drawn medium produces both readings.
Compromisso · cryptographic commitment
A cryptographic fingerprint, published beforehand, of something that is revealed only later — the sitter's answers, the medium's reading, the seed of the draw. Whoever checks at the end knows that nothing was swapped in between.
Desmascaramento · unblinding
The revelation of which reading was about which target. It happens on its own when the declared number of pairs is complete — nobody chooses the moment — and it is single and irreversible.
Pacote de verificação · verification package
The exported set that lets a third party audit the study and re-run the analysis: preregistration, seeds, allocations, anonymized assessments and audit trail.

Life cycle

What happens to a reading

  1. Request. The sitter gives the first name of the deceased person and answers a closed questionnaire about them. The answers lock with a salted commitment on the trail, before any medium receives the request.
  2. Structured reading. The drawn medium receives only the two first names of the pair and answers the same questionnaire for each one. On submission, the reading locks with a hash on the trail.
  3. Scoring. At the end of collection, each reading is compared field by field with the answers of the target's sitter and with those of the other sitter in the pair, the decoy. What counts is the difference between the two comparisons.
  4. Feedback. Each sitter sees the medium's answers for their own target alongside their own; each medium sees the counts for their own readings.

Who sees what

This is the project's central security specification, and every forbidden cell becomes an automated test that tries to violate it.

  • The medium sees only the first names in the requests they received — never the sitter, never the sitter's answers.
  • The sitter does not know which medium received their request, and does not see the reading before the end.
  • The administrator does not see answers, first names, readings or the link between a request and a medium before unblinding, and has no way to read the seed of the draw: it is held in encrypted custody.
  • The scores are not hidden: they simply do not exist before unblinding, and the database refuses to record them.

No artificial intelligence model takes part in evaluating the readings, in the scoring or in deciding a hit. Scoring is an equality comparison between closed options, under a published rule.
