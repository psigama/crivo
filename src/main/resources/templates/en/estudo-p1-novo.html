Preregistration of the study

Before any pairing, the study is declared and frozen. You write what is below; the rest — questionnaire, pairing rule, scoring rule, analysis and exclusion rules — comes from the protocol and goes into the document as it is (SPEC, E13).

Nothing is recorded while you fill this in. On freezing, the document receives a hash and the seed of the draw receives a commitment, and neither of them ever changes again.

The questionnaire in force is a draft (v1-rascunho). In production, a study only freezes the version reviewed by the researcher.

Error

The pairing stops at N active pairs; the unblinding comes when all of them have both readings.

No medium receives more pairs than this in the study, so that the result is not that of a single person. Between 1 and N.

Basis of the power calculation, which the frozen document declares.

Declared beforehand because selecting mediums by previous performance inflates the effect.
