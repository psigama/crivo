The most common questions of people thinking of taking part, in a few words. Tap a question to see the answer.

For anyone thinking of taking part

Do I have to be a spiritist, or have any religion?

No. Crivo does not take the side of any doctrine and does not interpret anything from a religious point of view. For mediums, each study may have criteria for taking part, declared before the collection begins.

Will I receive a message from the person who died?

No. The study is not a letter and not a message, and you do not ask the medium questions. The medium answers a multiple-choice questionnaire, and the system compares the answers. At the end, you see that comparison and the result of the whole study.

Will the medium know who I am?

No. The medium receives only the first name of the person who died, together with that of another person, and does not see who made the request or your answers. The two of you do not talk: everything happens through the website.

Why does the medium receive only the first name?

So that they have no way of looking the person up or of getting hints from anyone. The first name is the minimum for the reading to happen. It still gives away a few things, such as gender, and that is why requests are compared with others of a similar profile.

And if I do not know how to answer a question?

Mark "I don't know". It is a legitimate answer and better than a guess: the question leaves the comparison, and nobody is left with an advantage. The medium has the option "Nothing came", which also carries no penalty.

Do I need a computer?

No. A mobile phone with internet and an e-mail address are enough, to create the account and receive the notices.

When does the result come out?

Only when the study completes the number of pairs agreed at the start. That may take quite a long time, because it depends on how many people take part. Before that, no score exists at all, for anyone. When it comes out, you receive an e-mail.

Does the result say whether the medium is good?

No. The result belongs to the study, computed over dozens of pairs. One reading on its own says little, because chance explains many agreements. Crivo gives nobody a certificate of mediumship.

Who sees my answers?

Nobody on the team, before the end. The scoring is automatic. At the end, the answers are published so that anyone can redo the count, but only with codes: without your name, without your e-mail and without the first name of the person who died.

Can I withdraw?

Yes, at any time, without explaining why: you only have to delete your account. Your identifiable data are erased right away.

About Crivo

How do I know that nobody cheats, not even the team itself?

The rules of the study are written down and sealed before it begins. Each answer is sealed as soon as it is sent. The draw can be redone by anyone, and every step goes into a public record that nobody can alter, stamped with the date by an independent service. The details are in Guarantees and on the page for researchers.

Does Crivo want to prove that mediumship exists?

No, nor that it does not exist. Crivo neither asserts nor denies the phenomenon. It exists so that the answer, whatever it is, can be checked and defended. Every result is published, for or against.

What is the quick test?

They are two extrasensory perception tests, with no sign-up and with the result right away, which anyone can take. They say nothing about mediumship or about people who have died. See the tests.

Who is behind Crivo?

Crivo is maintained by PsiGama. The code is open, under the MIT license, on GitHub, and anyone can check it.
