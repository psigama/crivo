For researchers

A protocol that defends itself

The design of Crivo's mediumship experiment, P1-automated, on one page: what is blind, what is randomized, what is frozen before the first datum, which analysis runs, and how to check all of it without asking anyone for permission.

Status. P1 is implemented end to end but has not yet run with real participants. Sign-up opens after approval by a research ethics committee. The full specification, in Portuguese, is the SPEC, epic E13.

Design

Automated structured reading, in pairs

  • The sitter (consulente) gives the first name of the deceased person and answers a closed questionnaire about them. The answers lock with a salted commitment published on the audit trail before any medium receives the request.
  • Locked requests are paired within the same stratum: gender, age band and time since death. Each request is the decoy of the other.
  • A medium is drawn for the pair from the eligible mediums in canonical order and receives only the two first names, in a drawn order. The medium answers the same questionnaire for each name, and each reading locks with its own commitment.
  • The questionnaire in force, version v1, has 16 fields, of which 13 are scored; the others only serve the pairing. "Don't know" and "nothing received" never match anything. The questionnaire, field by field (in Portuguese).
  • Scoring is equality between closed options under a published rule. For each pair, d is, over both readings, the number of matches with the target minus the number of matches with the decoy. Nobody judges free text, and no AI model scores anything.
  • Chance hit rate: 1 in 2, choosing between target and decoy.

Before the first datum

Preregistration and randomization

  • The preregistration is frozen with a hash before the first pair: hypotheses, number of pairs (N), medium deadline (14 days by default), cap of pairs per medium (10 by default), expected effect and medium inclusion criteria, with the whole questionnaire in the canonical annex. Once frozen, the database refuses changes; amendments create a new version.
  • The seed is 32 bytes from the cryptographic generator, expanded with SHA-256 in counter mode. The commitment published in the frozen document is SHA-256(seed || "|" || canonical study input). The seed is kept encrypted and revealed at unblinding; with it, anyone can redo the draw of every medium and every name order, in any input order.
  • Expired deadline or a medium who leaves: the request goes to another drawn medium, and the locked readings of the expired assignment stay out of the analysis. Both exclusion rules are in the preregistration.

Analysis

Declared beforehand, run without choices

  • Binding stopping rule. When the N active pairs have both readings locked, the server's own cycle closes collection and unblinds. Nobody picks the moment, not even the administrator. Before that, no screen, API or projection exposes any function of the scores: progress is a count of units.
  • Primary: exact one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test on the pairs' d (exact up to 20 pairs with nonzero d; above that, normal approximation, and the report says which was used).
  • Secondary: exact binomial sign test of d; and a permutation of the readings across targets within the pairing strata (Pratt and Birge), exact while it fits the enumeration and, above that, with 100,000 arrangements drawn from the revealed seed.
  • The "confirmatory" label only holds if the preregistration was frozen and the sample is the declared one; otherwise the analysis is marked exploratory, with the reason. Item probabilities are never multiplied as if independent.
  • The report, hashed on the trail, carries the preregistration, the commitment and the revealed seed, the three analyses, the exclusions, the per-pair and per-medium tables, and what the result does not support.

Integrity

Blinding and a trail you can check from outside

  • Each role has its own projections: a forbidden field does not exist in the query. Every rule of the blinding matrix has a test that tries to break it (BlindingMatrixTest). No medium code enters the trail before unblinding.
  • The trail is append-only, chained with SHA-256, with update and delete blocked in the database. Its head is timestamped by FreeTSA (RFC 3161) after every milestone and every 24 hours if it changed; the tokens are at Carimbos de tempo, and airlab verify rechecks them.
  • At unblinding, each study's verification package, public and without names, lets anyone redo the trail, the draw, the scoring and the analysis; airlab verificar-pacote checks the file with no server and no database. Studies and reports are at Estudos, and the state of the trail at Verificar.

Declared limits

What a P1 result does not support

Straight from the protocol catalog, which requires a non-empty list from every protocol, so this page never drifts from the code:

  • Limite

In short: an above-chance result shows that information was there, not where it came from; there is no proxy experimenter; the closed questionnaire only captures what fits its fields; the first name leaks some information; and collusion between people who know each other cannot be ruled out with certainty. Crivo neither asserts nor denies the phenomenon, and a p-value is a statement about the test, not about the source of the information.
