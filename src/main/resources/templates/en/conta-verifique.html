One step left: confirm your e-mail

We sent a message to the address you gave. Open the link in it within 48 hours to activate the account. Without that confirmation, the account is not valid.

If the e-mail already had an account, the message says so, and nothing new is created. This page is the same in both cases, so that no one can find out from here who takes part.

Did not arrive? Check the spam folder. Once the deadline has passed, create the account again to receive another link.

Local profile. No e-mail leaves this machine. The confirmation link is in the application log, on a line that starts with Perfil local: link de confirmacao, and in the outbox, which only the administrator opens.

Local profile with SMTP. The e-mail was delivered to the configured SMTP server — with Mailpit, it shows up at localhost:8025 and does not leave the machine. The link is also in the application log, on a line that starts with Perfil local: link de confirmacao.
