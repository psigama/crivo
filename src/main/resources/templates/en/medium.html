For mediums

You are a medium and want to contribute. What happens.

This page is for you, who are thinking of taking part as a medium. It explains what will be asked of you, why each condition exists and what you receive at the end.

The conditions of the study exist to protect your honesty. You do not know who made the request, you see nobody and you receive only a first name. Because of that, if there is a hit, nobody will be able to say it was a trick, a search on the internet or reading someone's reactions.

Step by step

What will happen, in order

  1. Account and consent

    You create an account with the medium role and confirm your e-mail. The agreement says which data are kept and for how long.

    the role is fixed: a medium cannot be a sitter, in any study

  2. A request comes to you

    When the draw picks you, an e-mail arrives with the notice and the deadline, with no name at all. The names appear only on your dashboard, after you sign in.

  3. Two first names

    Each request carries the first names of two people who have died, in an order drawn by lot. The two come from two different sitters, with people of a similar profile, and each name serves as the comparison for the other. You do not know who made either request.

  4. You answer for each name

    For each name, you answer the same multiple-choice questionnaire the sitter answered: how the death happened, where the person lived, their temperament, some habits. In each question, choose an option or "Nothing came".

  5. Once locked, the reading is sealed

    Before you send it, you review everything. Once locked, the reading does not change any more, neither by you nor by the team.

    this protects the study from an adjustment after the fact, and protects you from the suspicion of having adjusted anything

  6. Nobody sees a score before the end

    While the study is collecting, no score exists at all: not for you, not for the sitter, not for whoever administers the platform. When the number of pairs agreed at the start is complete, the system itself opens the results.

  7. Your scores

    At the end, you receive an e-mail and see on your dashboard how many answers in each reading agreed with those of the right person and how many with those of the person for comparison, as well as the result of the whole study.

How to answer

What is asked of you

"Nothing came" is a legitimate answer, and there is no penalty. It simply does not count as a hit. A guess given only to fill in the blank counts as if it were information.

Answer with whatever comes for each name

Answer in the way you work, but within the options of the questionnaire. If nothing comes for a question, mark "Nothing came".

Respect the deadline

Each request has a deadline, agreed at the start of the study; the usual one is 14 days. If the deadline passes, the request goes to another medium drawn by lot, and the readings you had already locked in it stay out of the analysis.

Do not try to find out whose request it is

Do not ask anyone who made the request and do not talk about the names you received. The study is only worth something if you have no way of knowing whose each name is by another route.

A limit on requests

Each medium receives at most the number of pairs agreed at the start of the study; the usual one is 10. That way, the result does not depend on one person alone.

Your rights

What is guaranteed to you

Leave whenever you want

At any time, with no justification. If you delete your account with a request open, it goes to another medium, and your identifiable data are erased.

Your name stays out

In the research data you are a code. Your name and your e-mail are kept encrypted, in a separate part of the database. Before the end of the study, not even your code appears in the public record.

Nobody is exposed

The report and the verification package, published at the end, use only codes. The platform does not publish names, does not keep a public list of mediums and is nobody's showcase.

No machine judges

The scoring compares the options that were marked, by a published rule that anyone can redo. No artificial intelligence program reads or scores anything.

Respect for your practice

In your spiritist center or in your terreiro, this may have another name. Here, so as not to take sides, we call it a reading. The platform does not interpret anything from the point of view of any doctrine.

Honesty about the result

What this will not give you

It is not a certificate of mediumship, nor the opposite. The result belongs to the study, computed over dozens of pairs. One reading on its own says little: common answers, such as "died of a heart condition", agree with a great many people, and chance explains a good part of the agreements in any reading.

A positive result overall also does not decide where the information came from. And the most likely result, by what the literature shows to this day, is modest or null. Even so, one well-made study, whatever the result, is worth more than a thousand accounts nobody can check.
