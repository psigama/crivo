What will happen to your request

  1. Draft

    You give the first name and answer the questionnaire, taking your time, and you can review it.

  2. Locked

    On confirming, the answers are sealed and change no more. The study's public record receives only a digital seal of them, never the answers.

  3. Waiting for a pair

    The system joins your request to another with a similar profile; each one serves as a comparison for the other.

  4. Sent to the medium

    A medium drawn by lot receives only the first name and answers the same questionnaire.

  5. Waiting for the end of the study

    The checking only happens at the opening of the results, when the study reaches the declared number of pairs.

  6. Feedback

    You see the medium's answers alongside yours, and the score.
