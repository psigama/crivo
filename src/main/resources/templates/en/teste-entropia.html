Why this settles the matter of trust: to make you miss, the server would have to choose its half after knowing yours — and it has already published the hash of its own part. Finding another half that fits your draw and yields the desired result is a preimage attack on SHA-256. Nobody knows how to do that.

Your browser also stores the commitment above, so it can check at the end whether the half that is revealed really is this one. None of this leaves your computer.
