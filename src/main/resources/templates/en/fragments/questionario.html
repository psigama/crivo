The questionnaire, version v1-rascunho

The sitter and the medium answer the same fields, and the comparison is field by field, between closed options, with nobody judging free text. There are 16 fields: 13 go into the score and 3 serve only to form the pairs.

This version is a draft, awaiting the researcher's review. No study is frozen with it in production, and it may change before the first study.

In the fields that go into the score, the sitter may answer "I don't know" and the medium, "I received nothing". Those answers never match anything. The pairing fields are required for the sitter and are not asked of the medium.
