A figure has already been drawn for this trial. Before seeing it, write down what comes to you: shapes, colors, movement, a sense of place. Describe, do not try to guess the name of a thing.

Locking the impression…

Once submitted, the impression is locked: the system stores its hash and does not accept a rewrite. That is what makes it possible to state afterwards that you did not adjust the text after seeing the images.
