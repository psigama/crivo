Four protocols: two experiments and two tests

Crivo is a platform of blind protocols for research into anomalous cognition, of two kinds. The experiments are about mediumship — in the literature, anomalous information reception, or AIR —: they involve a sitter and a medium, require an account, and only have a result at the end of a preregistered study. The tests are about extrasensory perception: the target is drawn by the system itself, no deceased person is involved, and the result comes out right away.

What every protocol here has in common: a draw with a commitment published beforehand and revealed afterwards, a target out of reach of whoever answers, and an audit trail chained by hash and timestamped. A protocol that cannot offer this does not enter the catalog. The experiments add a frozen preregistration and automatic unblinding at the end of the study; the tests are anonymous, exploratory sessions, with the result at the end of the session.
