P4 · before you begin

Remote viewing with four targets

In each trial you write your impression before seeing any figure. Only then do four appear — one is the target, three are decoys — for you to rank from the one most like your impression to the one least like it.

  • The impression is locked before the figures appear. The system stores its hash. Without that, anyone could rewrite the impression after seeing the images — it is the standard objection to anyone judging their own hit.
  • A hit is the target ranked first. Chance level: 25%.
  • The figure pool is versioned, and the version used is recorded in the session. Changing the figures later does not change what was analyzed.
  • No results along the way, and the seed is revealed at the end.

The limit of this protocol, stated plainly: the person judging the similarity is you. That reintroduces the "how is it scored" criticism, which forced choice does not have. It is the price of the free-response format, and it is why this test exists alongside the other one, not instead of it.
