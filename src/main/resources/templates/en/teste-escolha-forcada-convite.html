P3 · before you begin

Precognitive forced choice

In each trial you pick one of four doors. The target for that trial is only computed after your answer is locked — at the moment of the choice it does not yet exist anywhere.

  • No results along the way. During the session you see how many trials are left, never how many you got right. If you could see that, you could stop at a good moment — and then the final number would be worth nothing.
  • The number of trials is fixed now and does not change afterwards.
  • At the end, the seed is revealed. With it you recompute every target on your own and check that none was chosen after your answer.
  • No personal data. The session is anonymous: no account, no e-mail.

An honest expectation: the large replications of Bem's precognition findings came out null (Ritchie, Wiseman and French, 2012; Galak et al., 2012). Chance level here is 25%. This test exists to demonstrate the verification method, not to promise an effect.
